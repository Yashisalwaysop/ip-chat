Why IP-Chat

Built for privacy, not profiles.

No databases. No cookies. No breadcrumbs. Just a room code and a conversation.

01 | No Account

Zero Sign-Up

ANONYMOUS INSTANT PRIVATE

No email. No password. No identity. Just pick a room code and you're in — as nobody. There's nothing to hack because there's nothing to store.

02 | No Logs

No History

RAM-ONLY VOLATILE ZERO-DISK

Messages live in RAM, not on disk. Nothing is written, saved, or recoverable. Ever.

03 | Ephemeral

Self-Destructs

AUTO-PURGE TRANSIENT GRACEFUL

When the last person leaves, a countdown begins. Then the room and everything in it cease to exist.
